A Place Where My Heart Can Rest

On choosing someone, even when the story isn’t perfect.

Unknown User

4 min read · Sep 28, 2026

Everyone keeps asking me about how much I love her. Maybe we remain in silence, but did you know we started it all with happiness? Guess what, we love each other. It’s very simple, yet somehow it means everything to me.

She became a place where my heart could finally rest. A place where I don’t have to pretend to be someone else, where silence doesn’t feel empty, and where even the smallest moments can feel special. Being with her feels like coming home after a long, tiring journey.

I don’t know what the future will bring us. Maybe there will be days when we barely speak, days when we misunderstand each other, or days when everything feels difficult. But one thing I know for sure is that my feelings for her were never about having a perfect story. They were about choosing her, even when things were not perfect.

I love the way she makes ordinary moments feel meaningful. I love the way her presence can calm my mind without even trying. And sometimes, I don’t need her to say anything. Just knowing that she is there is already enough to make me feel at peace.

Maybe I will never find the perfect words to explain how much she means to me. But if love is about finding a place where your heart feels safe, then she will always be that place for me. and I know it wasnt same anymore.

But The Writer wants you to know that I always love you.
